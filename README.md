# Fittest
> A minimalist workout builder that creates fitness programs based on workout duration, type, and access to equipment.

## Overview
As a student in college, I found it difficult to get to the gym and even harder to figure out what to *do* once I got there. Enter **Fittest**, the workout generator that takes the hassle out of fitness planning. All you have to provide is exercise duration, weekly frequency, exercise types (i.e. pilates, cardio), and equipment access. **Fittest** handles the rest, making it easy to start and stick to working out, whether you're in a college dorm or at the gym.

## Features
- **Dual Themes:** Light and dark mode supported for early-morning and late-night planning.
- **Large exercise library:** Access to a large database of exercises to pull from to ensure variety and progression.
- **Exportable Programs:** Easily save your new program as a .docx document to print out or download to your device.
